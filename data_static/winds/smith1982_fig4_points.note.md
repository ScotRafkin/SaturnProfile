# smith1982_fig4_points.csv

Source: Smith, B. A., et al. 1982, Science 215, 504, Fig. 4 (left panel). Caption: "Zonal
(eastward) winds in the reference frame of Saturn's magnetic field (5). Each point refers
to a single cloud feature that has been followed for one Saturnian rotation. Points from
7 deg to 20 deg S are from Voyager 1 (8)." Latitude axis is planetographic (Lindal et al.
1985, Appendix: the published zonal-wind data "give V_w as a function of the planetographic
latitude"). Frame: System III (the magnetic field frame). Positive eastward.

Digitized 10 September 2026 by the script beside this file from the journal PDF at 600 dpi;
see the script docstring for the method and the overlay PNG for the check. 323 points.
Coverage: about 73 S to 80 N; no points between roughly 7 S and 3 N other than the equatorial
cluster near 5 N, and none poleward of 80 N or 73 S. Per-point reading error about 2.5 m/s
and 0.8 deg; points recovered from merged clusters by area splitting carry a larger latitude
error, up to about 2 deg in the densest clusters (near 38 N, 43 S, 72 N).

This is the raw digitization. The model-input wind file is built from it by the wind tool
(binned mean and standard deviation, planetographic to planetocentric conversion, altitude
independence per Lindal), never read by the model directly.
